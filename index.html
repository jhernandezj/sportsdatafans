Famous matches, seasons and records, replayed with data

Sports Data Fans is a YouTube channel of short vertical videos (YouTube Shorts) that turn open sports data into animated stories: a World Cup final minute by minute, a title race matchday by matchday, a record chase stat by stat.

What we publish

Minute by MinuteFamous matches replayed as they happened: goals, cards, chances and the stats at every break.
Title RacesWhole seasons of football and Formula 1, round by round.
Records & LegendsCareers and head-to-heads compared with the numbers.
Guess WhoQuizzes: guess the player or team from six numbers.

Where the data comes from

Only open data whose licence allows reuse: openfootball (CC0), international results (CC0), F1DB (CC BY 4.0), nflverse (CC BY 4.0) and Wikidata (CC0). Match facts such as chances and statistics are taken from the match reports on Wikipedia and credited in each video. Music comes from the YouTube Audio Library.

Sports Data Fans Publisher

The channel is run with a small private tool, Sports Data Fans Publisher. It runs on the channel owner's own computer and is used by nobody else. It uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload the channel's own finished videos with their title, description and tags, and schedule when they go public;
  • add each video to the channel's playlists (by format and by sport);
  • check whether scheduled videos have been published;
  • read the channel's own YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, subscribers) to decide what to make next.

The only account that ever signs in is the channel owner's. The tool does not access any other user's data, does not show YouTube content to anyone and is not offered to the public. How it handles data is explained in the Privacy Policy, and its use is governed by the Terms of Service.
